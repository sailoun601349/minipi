3.2 Removing higher-order terms

To minimize the loss L(θ), we expand it around the current parameter θt and keep only the first-order term. The second-order term is dropped because its contribution is O(α²) and vanishes as the step size α → 0. Consequently the update rule reduces to θt+1 = θt − α ∇L(θt).

This is the standard justification given in most textbooks. It silently assumes that L is twice continuously differentiable and that the Hessian is well conditioned along the direction of the gradient.
